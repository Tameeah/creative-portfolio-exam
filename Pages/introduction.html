The Creative Portfolio

Introduction

The Creative Portfolio shows a collection of artworks created by Tameeah Padayachy.

This portfolio shows a variety of media, subjects and techniques which she has explored over the years. The Creative Portfolio display's her strongest pieces and highlights the type of artist she is.

Throughout this portfolio, you will learn Tameeah as an artist, the type of palette she gravitates towards, trying to bring her vision to life.

With the power of her art she aims to bring more love into the world.
